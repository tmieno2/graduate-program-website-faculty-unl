ARD Funding decision policy

The graduate committee is responsible for allocating departmental funding to graduate students each academic year. This document outlines the criteria and process used to make funding decisions.

Decision process

  1. The graduate committee distributes a survey to all faculty asking them to indicate funding needs for each of their current students. Any requests that were submitted later than the deadline will be placed at the bottom of priority.
  2. The committee removes ineligible requests (see Eligibility above) and determines whether the available ARD funding is sufficient to cover all remaining requests.
  3. If funding is insufficient, requests are ranked according to the allocation criteria above and funded in priority order until the budget is exhausted.
  4. If ARD funds remain after covering all eligible requests, the committee makes offers to the current pool of incoming applicants within the available budget.

Earning an ARD credit

Sometimes, you cannot identify an applicant who fits well with your research interest even though you have funding to cover a graduate student. If the founder of your project allows for it, you can pay for the current graduate student supported by ARD and earn 1 year GRA credit. You can use that credit to pay for a year of 1 GRA when you need it. This is only when the student will not work for your project (pure monetary swapping).

Expectation for bridge funding when bringing a new student to the program

Years of funding secured ARD bridge funding provided Permitted to recruit a new student
1 year None No
2 years Up to 2 years No
3 years 1 year Yes
4 years Possible additional year Yes

1 year of funding secured

If you have only one year of funding guaranteed and enroll a student, the department will NOT provide ARD funding for the remainder of his/her program under any circumstances. Faculty may not recruit graduate students with the expectation that the department will assume long-term financial responsibility for their support. As an alternative, you may identify a current student in need of funding to work for you, or arrange for a student currently supported by ARD to be covered by your funding, thereby earning an ARD credit to apply toward future departmental support for your student.

2 years of funding secured

If you have two years of funding guaranteed for an incoming student, the department will provide bridge funding for up to 2 years. However, you will NOT be permitted to recruit any additional new students using your own funding, as those resources should be directed toward supporting your current student.

3 years of funding secured

If you have three years of funding guaranteed for an incoming student, the department is most likely able to provide bridge funding for 1 year as such a student is a top priority as shown below in the table. You are permitted to recruit a new student using your own funding, as you will have provided the student sufficient financial support.

4 years of funding secured

If you have four years of funding guaranteed for an incoming student, the student is in a strong position to receive an additional year of ARD funding if necessary. You are permitted to recruit a new student using your own funding.

Allocation criteria

The graduate committee uses the following criteria when allocating fellowships. These are considered holistically.
